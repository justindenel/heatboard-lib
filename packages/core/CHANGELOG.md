### Patch Changes

## 0.1.0 — Initial release

- Bracket actions through `applyAction`, with validation, and a language-neutral error model.
- Formats by rounds: plans, repechage, small final, seeds, double elimination, JSON import and export.
- Scoring with categories, figures and presets, plus head-judge and free judging modes.
- Provisional and final standings, simulated rounds, and read-only viewer results.
- Heat timer, breaks and automatic chaining.
